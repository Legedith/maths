# Verification of the Boolean-rank connections

The independent product audit accepted the scoped integration of seven
concepts, eight relations and two learning journeys. The combined curated map
contains 40 concepts and 52 relations. Its conclusion applies to the source,
data projection, validation and server-rendered/API behavior identified in
the [audit report](../evidence/boolean-rank/independent/final-boolean-rank-integration-audit.json).

The reviewer compared all 49 files in the frozen implementation with the
actual checkout. Independent checks covered the admitted mathematical
records, preservation of earlier corpus records, malformed inputs, complete
connection objects, rendered conditions and local HTTP responses. Canonical
functional, navigation and targeted Python checks also passed. Earlier
reviewer-checker failures and their corrections remain in the evidence packet.

The reviewer previously supplied integration-design advice, but did not
write the product implementation. The separate source-admission review is
retained. These role boundaries and the precise limits of the checks are
recorded in the report; neither author agreement nor structural validation is
treated as a proof of source entailment.

The [evidence bundle](../.codex/evidence/runs/boolean-rank-integration-v1/bundle.json)
links claims to the frozen inputs and raw results. Reproduce the product
check with `node scripts/check-boolean-rank.mjs --output work/boolean-rank-functional.json`
from the repository root, using a fresh output path. A final production build
is recorded separately during release, after documentation and evidence are
finalized.

This verifies a bounded integration of known mathematics. It establishes no
new theorem, automatic semantic matching, retrieval accuracy, visual or
responsive quality, deployed behavior, or measured real-world benefit. The
separate teaching example, role-mining evaluation and candidate Kemeny proof
are outside this release's evidence gate.
