# MSC2020 data attribution and terms

The classification data in this directory and the source snapshots in `experiments/msc-index/inputs/raw/` are adapted from **Mathematics Subject Classification 2020**, jointly maintained by **Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH**, and from the **MSC2020 SKOS** conversion by Susanne Arndt, Patrick Ion, Mila Runnwerth, Moritz Schubotz and Olaf Teschke, published in the TIBHannover/MSC2020_SKOS repository.

These classification data and their JSON adaptation are distributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International**, with the retained [license text](LICENSE-CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0.md) and [canonical license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). This notice does not assign those data terms to separately authored application or adapter code. No source endorsement is implied.

Sources: [official MSC2020](https://msc2020.org/), [official tab-separated download](https://msc2020.org/MSC_2020.csv), [pinned SKOS revision](https://github.com/TIBHannover/MSC2020_SKOS/tree/33972ddb6a72c3660a6e499ee5f881b57fa92d41), and [conversion methodology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.13877).

Changes: conversion to JSON navigation records; parent derivation from classification-code structure; inclusion of source cross-references, scopes and collection members; explicit flags for label and hierarchy disagreements. Original source bytes, labels, descriptions, RDF terms and conditions remain available in the retained input/output records. No mathematical equivalence or completeness claim is introduced.

The source-manifest records other local research snapshots for provenance. Those full webpage/procedure snapshots are not part of this portable source package and are not needed by the importer.
