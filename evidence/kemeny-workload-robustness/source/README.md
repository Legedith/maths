# Established methods and unresolved overlap

The [source notes](source-notes.md) record four bounded search queries, two new
source opens and reused source audits. The
[independent source review](../bound-review/source-review.md) inspected the
actual retained primary passages and access error. Public hashes identify
those local raw returns; bulk publisher/arXiv text is not copied into Git.

The weighted commute/resistance connection, complete-multipartite inverse
formulas and exact single-edge inverse updates are established. The earlier
[source assessment](../../kemeny-uniform-repair/source/source-notes.md) gives
the exact Chandra et al., Bapat et al. and Monnig-Meyer locators and normalization
conventions. Monnig-Meyer explicitly discuss optimal addition using the exact
update; their Kirchhoff index counts ordered pairs.

[Ghosh, Boyd and Saberi](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/eff_res.pdf)
study normalized conductance optimization (equations2/26), inverse-based
convexity (section2.7) and broader weighted resistance objectives (section8).
A fixed covariance trace is a sum of such inverse quadratic forms, an algebraic
inference from this machinery. It is not a claim that their paper proves the
present family's discrete repair ordering.

[Zelazo and Burger](https://arxiv.org/html/1408.4471), sectionIII-A and
sectionV-A TheoremV.5/CorollaryV.6, treat additive uncertainty in consensus
networks. Their inverse-effective-resistance margins concern consensus
stability. They are not margins for preserving the best added link under a
fixed workload, and they must not be substituted into this decision problem.

[Sardar's analytical study of resistance and Kirchhoff perturbations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0960077925009105)
is especially close generic sensitivity work: the primary publisher preview
describes changing one existing unit weight and exact resistance/Kirchhoff
effects. The full publisher open failed. Exact theorem domains and overlap
with discrete best-insertion stability remain unresolved. A preview cannot
clear publication priority. The abstract-only Hessian lead in the notes is
also not treated as an inspected theorem.

The rational-update quadratic and the sufficient Loewner gap inequality are
direct algebraic consequences of established methods, not new generic
algorithms. Simultaneous relative perturbations and a single additive attack
are different uncertainty sets. Candidate final conductance is common only
because the same old perturbation and unit insertion are used in each
comparison; this cancellation does not cover doing nothing.

The previous [join and optimization assessment](../../kemeny-weighted-repair/source/README.md)
also remains relevant. The damaged graph can be represented as a graph join;
it is not valid to exclude join-formula overlap merely because it is no longer
complete multipartite. Fulltext gaps and final-version comparisons remain.
No bounded search establishes global originality. The possible contribution
is the family-specific full decision rule and explicitly scoped certificates
and counterexamples; that contribution needs further priority assessment.
