# Known methods and bounded overlap assessment

These sources establish the general machinery. The possible contribution is the particular complete ordering of insertion orbits in the damaged three-part-plus-hub family, not a new electrical or inverse-update method. The inspected sources did not identify that exact ordering; this is a bounded non-identification result, not proof of originality or historical openness.

- Chandra, Raghavan, Ruzzo, Smolensky and Tiwari, [The electrical resistance of a graph captures its commute and cover times](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~ruzzo/papers/resist.pdf), Theorem2.1. For a simple undirected graph with m edges, H_st+H_ts=2m R_st. Consequently, with zero diagonal and independent uniform start/target, U=(2m/n^2)Kf=(2m/n)tr(L+), where Kf sums unordered pairs. This counts walk steps. Conditioning on distinct pairs changes the common normalization, not fixed-n rankings.

- Bapat, Karimi and Liu, [Kirchhoff index and degree Kirchhoff index of complete multipartite graphs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.09457), 2016 v1 preprint: Section2 Corollary2.6 (printed p4), Section3 Proposition3.3 and Corollary3.5 (printed pp5-7), Section4 Theorem4.2 (printed pp7-8). These give resistance, inverse/spectral and Kirchhoff formulas for intact complete multipartite graphs and extremization at fixed n,r. They are antecedents for intact-base calculations. The target damaged graph and non-restoration outcomes are not complete multipartite; that constrained insertion problem is not the theorem's feasible set. Section5's degree Kirchhoff index is a different objective. The final published revision was not exhaustively compared.

- Monnig and Meyer, [The Resistance Perturbation Distance: A Metric for the Analysis of Dynamic Networks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.01091), v2: Definition3/equation24 (printed p9), Theorem2/equation37 (printed p13), Section8.1 (printed pp20-21), AppendixB.1. Exact single-edge resistance/Kirchhoff updates and their use to choose an optimal addition are explicit prior work. Their Kirchhoff convention sums ordered pairs and is twice the unordered Kf above. Section8.2 is a local greedy approximation, distinct from the exact score. Generic rank-one scoring and Kirchhoff-optimal edge search must be credited as known; no inspected passage states the target family-wide orbit ordering.

- Abbas and Egerstedt, [Robust Graph Topologies for Networked Systems](https://repository.gatech.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/a34a558e-8c73-4469-8866-48012421c2da/content), Section3 Theorem5 (fourth PDF page), followed by Section4. Their exact one-edge optimum concerns the coalesced triangle-petal/star family (F1)^k bullet S_m, m>=2, and connects two degree-one vertices. This is family-specific edge-design prior work, but it does not directly cover the present damaged multipartite graph, whose minimum degree is at least six. This comparison uses retained primary fulltext passages; the fresh institutional open failed.

For equal unit additions to the same H, final n and edge count agree, so U and Kf rank candidates identically. Comparing to no action changes the edge count; Kirchhoff decrease alone does not establish U improvement. Source attribution does not certify the new orbit inequalities or no-op comparison: those require their separate mathematical proofs.
