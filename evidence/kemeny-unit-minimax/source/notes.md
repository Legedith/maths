## Transfer and attribution limits

The proposed endpoint reduction remains appropriately described as an elementary linear-fractional inference. For positive affine L_e, L_j, the derivative of L_e/L_j has constant sign; maximize each pair at an uncertainty endpoint and commute finite maxima. This argument is supplied by the mathematical packet, not claimed as a newly discovered general method or quoted as a theorem from the inaccessible textbook passages. Likewise, comparing two endpoint regrets by positive cross multiplication yields the product criterion; its algebra alone is not an originality claim.

Neither inspected primary model establishes the required damaged-multipartite objective formulas, full action-set domination, tied optimal repair sets, or the family-specific midpoint counterexample. Those conclusions require their separate exact proofs and independent audits. The source check does not establish that such results are absent elsewhere. It does not establish first priority or physical benefit. S1 also makes a generic claim that point estimates can give high regret in its own experiments; those experiments are not evidence for this family's midpoint witness or real-world performance.
