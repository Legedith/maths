# Deterministic unit-repair minimax evidence

The [frozen contract](../../docs/kemeny-unit-minimax-contract.md),
[result note](../../docs/kemeny-unit-minimax.md) and
[typed bundle](../../.codex/evidence/runs/kemeny-unit-minimax-v1/bundle.json)
define the scope. Source, author, implementation and review artifacts are kept
separate. Original author reports retain their historical pending-review wording;
the subsequent distinct review packets provide the current acceptance evidence.

| Packet | Author | Independent reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Unequal unit domination | General | Ranking |
| Positive-affine endpoint lemma | Ranking | General |
| Complete family rule and midpoint witness | General | Damage |
| Failed strength extension and counterexample | General | Ranking |
| Bounded primary-source investigation | Ranking | Damage |

The mathematical packets are copied byte-for-byte from isolated D stages;
math-copy-manifest.json records original paths and hashes. Their evaluators,
raw streams, exact results and failed exploratory attempts remain unchanged.
Historical scripts may use local paths and are provenance; the portable
entry point is under experiments/kemeny-workload-proof.

[Source notes](source/notes.md) summarize inspected primary passages.
[Source review](source-review/review.md) independently checks support and access
limits. source/requests.json retains original structured requests and hashes
of full request/return files, which remain local under the recorded D paths.
Bulk third-party source text is not copied into public Git. The packet contains
four queries, three primary opens and four targeted finds. No bounded search
establishes priority or absence of prior work.

Implementation, root canonical logs and an independent implementation replay
are recorded separately from earlier exploratory runs. The final integration
review checks claim/code/log/source agreement. The deterministic gate checks
hashes, locators and provenance structure; it does not replace that review.
Actual hosted CI must be checked separately for the final pushed commit.

No action, variable-strength optimization, randomized and adaptive policies
are outside the main classification. The arbitrary-strength example is only a
boundary counterexample. Novelty, measured physical benefit and comprehensive
mathematical coverage remain unresolved.
