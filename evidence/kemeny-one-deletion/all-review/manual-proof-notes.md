# Manual semantic checks (before symbolic verdict)

Nine categories are exhaustive: original edges lie in A-B, A-C, B-C, A-h, B-h or C-h. Splitting A into {u,v} and A-rest splits A-B, A-C, A-h into two categories each, yielding nine. Within-part permutations fixing the unordered inserted pair act transitively on each category. Swapping B and C maps exactly uC,rC,Ch to uB,rB,Bh. The domain b>=a,c>=a is invariant under this swap, so no b<=c constraint is required. A-rest is nonempty because a>=3. Thus these representatives cover every original edge for every fixed prospective pair, not just some optimal pair or a worst deletion.

For each representative, distinguish u,v,h and any additional deletion endpoints. Residual vertices in each original part have identical neighborhoods, remain independent, and have equal degree both before and after uv is added. Cell-constant functions form an invariant subspace. Each residual-cell sum-zero subspace is killed by P, since every outside row is constant across that cell and the inside adjacency block is zero. For all positive cell sizes these subspaces give a direct decomposition and n-m omitted zero eigenvalues.

Only rB and rh can have an empty residual cell: A-rest has a-3 vertices. At a=3 the formal residual column of R is zero, and its diagonal entry is zero because the cell is independent. Its row degree is b+c+1>0 (the residual row retains all B,C,h connections; deleted r is distinguished). Therefore the corresponding column of I-Q is the coordinate unit vector. Expanding its characteristic determinant along this column gives an exact factor (1-lambda) for the normalized Laplacian characteristic polynomial, and deleting row/column leaves the genuine quotient. Equivalently Q has an artificial zero transition eigenvalue. The true quotient has m-1 cells: K_full=n-(m-1)+K_true=n-m+(K_true+1), precisely the formal formula. B-rest and C-rest have at least 2 vertices when split, and all other residual sizes are positive. No division by the empty cell size occurs; only its positive row degree is inverted.

After any original edge is deleted, its endpoints still have a length-two path through a third original part. Thus the graph stays connected, and adding uv preserves connectedness. Undirected random walk is diagonally similar to a symmetric matrix; 1 is simple and all remaining transition eigenvalues are less than 1. The genuine quotient spectrum is inherited from this invariant direct decomposition. The artificial zero mode at the boundary contributes normalized Laplacian eigenvalue 1. All degree diagonal factors are positive. Consequently F(t)=det(D)*t*product_(i=2..m)(t+mu_i) with mu_i>0, yielding q1>0 and q2/q1=sum 1/mu_i. The same n-m correction applies to both edge states and cancels.

A rational cancellation does not create a proof loophole if exact cross multiplication is verified and both original q1 factors and saved D are nonzero throughout the domain. Original factors are positive by the spectrum above; saved D is positive by its shifted coefficient certificate. Then N/D equals the physical difference everywhere, including a=3. All-positive coefficients with positive constant imply strict positivity on the entire nonnegative orthant, and x=a-3,y=b-a,z=c-a covers precisely the admitted integer triples. The result concerns exactly one original-edge deletion and the specified prospective pair; it asserts neither multiple failures nor optimal insertion/deletion ordering.
