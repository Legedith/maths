# Suggested attribution boundary

Single-edge strength tuning uses the classical weighted commute identity and exact rank-one Laplacian update. Conductance-allocation optimization is also established: [Ghosh, Boyd and Saberi, equation2, printed p38](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/eff_res.pdf) optimize effective resistance with normalized total conductance. Our variable-volume parameterization is equivalent, after common scaling, to a one-dimensional affine slice of that framework with old edge-weight ratios fixed. Thus changing total conductance does not by itself separate this problem from fixed-budget prior work.

The explicit scalar critical point comes from differentiating the known rational objective. Four bounded searches did not identify an earlier statement of the exact single-edge radical or the particular graph-family joint optimum, but that is not a priority certificate. Full coordinate-restricted prior results and earlier partial-access leads remain unresolved. Potential contribution claims should be limited to separately verified exact family ordering/global optimum results, with generic formulas and optimization machinery credited as known.
